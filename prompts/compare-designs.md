---
description: Compare design alternatives and their tradeoffs
argument-hint: "[design question or component]"
---
Compare designs for ${ARGUMENTS:-the design question in the current context}.

Present two or three meaningfully different alternatives. For each, describe the interface or structure, benefits, costs, risks, and when it is the best fit. Compare the alternatives against the requirements and constraints already known, then recommend one with a concise rationale and identify any decision that still needs user input.

Do not require subagents or a persistent planning process. Do not implement any alternative unless the user asks for implementation separately.
